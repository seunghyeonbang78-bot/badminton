THE STORY

Why I Built RacketLab

RacketLab started with a simple problem I experienced myself.

When I wanted to buy a new badminton racket, I searched everywhere — YouTube, Reddit, review websites, and forums. I watched countless reviews and compared specifications, but I still couldn't find a place where I could clearly see how different rackets actually compare to each other.

I eventually bought a new racket, but I realized that I still didn't have a simple way to see all of its important characteristics in one place or determine how well it matched my playing style.

“Why isn't there a website that organizes all of this information in one place?”

So I decided to build one myself.

RacketLab is my attempt to make choosing a badminton racket simpler, more transparent, and more personalized.

Instead of telling you which racket is the “best,” RacketLab focuses on showing you what each racket is designed to do and how its characteristics compare to others.

Spend less time searching. Understand your options faster. Find the racket that fits you.
